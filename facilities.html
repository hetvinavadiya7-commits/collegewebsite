01 / Find your focus

The library

A welcoming place to read, research, work with classmates, or find a quiet corner to think. Our library brings books, digital resources, and helpful staff together under one roof.

  • Study areas for individuals and groups
  • Print and digital learning resources
  • Friendly research support
02 / Build and explore

Computer labs

Get hands-on with the tools and technology used across your courses. Our dedicated lab spaces support practical learning, collaboration, and experimentation.

  • Computer workstations and internet access
  • Spaces for supervised practical sessions
  • Student-friendly technical support
03 / Move together

Sports & recreation

Make time to move, try a team sport, or cheer on your friends. Campus recreation makes it easy to stay active and get to know people outside class.

  • Outdoor courts and recreation areas
  • Student clubs and friendly games
  • Activities for a range of interests
04 / Take a breather

The canteen

Your go-to spot for a coffee between classes, a quick bite, or a long catch-up with friends. Pull up a chair and make yourself comfortable.

  • Casual seating and friendly service
  • Fresh snacks and drinks
  • A relaxed place to recharge
