Our story

Small beginnings.
Big horizons.

Northstar College began in 2001 with a simple belief: every student deserves the chance to find their strengths and shape their own future.

What started as a close-knit learning community has grown into a welcoming college where students bring different experiences, perspectives, and ambitions to the same table. Through every new program and every new class, we’ve held on to what matters most: personal attention, practical learning, and a genuine sense of belonging.

Today, Northstar is still a place to ask questions, try something new, and discover how far your ideas can take you.
